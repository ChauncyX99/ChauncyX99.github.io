Cheng Xuan was born in 1999 in Wuhan, China. After years of study in psychology, he has mastered a variety of research methods and published several academic papers. Currently, he is a PhD student at the School of Education, Renmin University of China. He is interested in the psychological and behavioral problems and resilience of adolescents, particularly the impact of real and online experiences on them. Through a systemic, developmental and positive lens, he hopes to explore the mechanisms involved and develops evidence-based prevention and intervention practices.
